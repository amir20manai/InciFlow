# PRODUCT.md

## Product
InciFlow is an incident and service management web application for internal operations teams. It helps employees report issues, administrators monitor activity across the organization, and technicians follow up on assigned tasks.

## Core user groups
- Admins: oversee all incident activity, dashboards, categories, departments, user management, and notifications.
- Employees: submit new incidents, track their own reports, and review updates.
- Technicians: work assigned interventions, consult incident history, and monitor operational notifications.

## Primary workflows
- Authentication and authorization with role-based access.
- Incident submission and detail review.
- Department and category configuration.
- Assignment and status handling for operational follow-up.
- Statistical reporting and overview dashboards.
- Notification delivery for recent activity.

## Product goals
- Reduce time between issue reporting and resolution.
- Make operational status visible at a glance to management.
- Keep incident records structured and actionable.
- Support role-specific experiences without losing cross-team visibility.

## User context
This is a business operations dashboard used in a professional work environment. Users need fast scanability, clear status grouping, and dependable data presentation more than decorative visual excess.

## Visual direction to preserve
- Clean enterprise SaaS aesthetic.
- Strong blue-led palette with neutral surfaces.
- Clear hierarchy, readable typography, and card-based layout.
- Task-oriented dashboards with dashboard metrics and operational summaries.

## Project constraints
- Angular 22 front-end application.
- Route-based separation by role.
- Existing data model is structured around incidents, departments, categories, and notifications.
- The UI should remain calm, professional, and operationally legible across desktop and tablet widths.

## Success criteria
- Users can understand incident volume and urgency immediately.
- Administrative dashboards provide quick operational decisions without dense visual noise.
- The interface feels trustworthy, efficient, and professional.
